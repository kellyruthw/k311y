# About

**Section label:** About

## The short version

I'm Kelly Warpechowski, a design engineer with 12+ years of shipping as an IC. I design products and marketing sites, architect the content systems behind them, and build the whole thing in code. Design and engineering usually get treated as two careers. I've spent 12 years treating them as one.

## The long version

I've been making things on the internet since Neopets pages and MySpace layouts. I was the kind of kid who learned CSS because the default profile wasn't *me* enough. That never stopped; it just started invoicing.

The career runs the whole stack of the web's last decade-plus: junior web designer, then editorial sites for Lionsgate and celebrity clients pulling 1M+ views a month, then marketing design and frontend where I learned that data has opinions (heatmaps informed redesigns that cut the bounce rate by 30%). Then product design: a desktop app connecting fans to celebrities, TV microsites for Amazon and Focus Features, a Redbox experience that converted 30k+ users in its first week, and a political app that a Product Manager and I pitched into an investment from the CEO of Toms.

Then the founding-designer era: first at So Yummy, designing CMS features with the editorial team that had to use them, and then at TerraTrue, where I was the founding designer on a 0-to-1 privacy platform. Design system, product, branding, all of it.

For the last 8+ years, I've also been the technical half of a long-running agency partnership, building headless WordPress sites for luxury hospitality and real estate brands. They're the kind of properties you drive past and recognize. I run that practice through my studio, Heartcore, alongside my own shop, Palette Town, where I sell hand-drawn Pokémon-inspired art prints on a headless Shopify stack I built, complete with its own admin panel and AI editorial pipeline.

## What drives it

I moved to Los Angeles for the creative energy, that feeling that everyone around you is writing the next script or building the next pitch deck. That's the current I work best in: cities, momentum, people making things. My idea of a great problem is one where the design, the code, and the content system all have to be solved *together*. That's the whole job, and it's the only version of it I've ever wanted.
